## Project Overview

This project was completed as part of the AnalystLab Africa Data Analytics Internship.

The objective was to analyze ShopSmart Retail Ltd.'s sales, inventory, customer, and returns data to identify business insights and support data-driven decision-making, identifying:

- Sales trends
- Inventory shortages
- Product return patterns
- Store performance

## Business Objectives

- Identify sales fluctuations throughout the year
- Determine inventory shortages across store locations
- Assess product return rates and their causes
- Evaluate store performance

## Methodology

### Data Cleaning and Preparation
- Combined multiple CSV datasets into a single workbook
- Established relationships between tables using XLOOKUP
- Cleaned and validated data by removing duplicates and standardizing date and currency formats. 


### Analysis
- Monthly revenue trend analysis
- Product performance analysis
- Store performance analysis
- Inventory availability assessment
- Return rate analysis

### Reporting
- Designed an executive dashboard
- Created a business report
- Generated actionable business insights and recommendations

### Assumptions  
- Discounts are usually given as percentages by retail stores, so all the discounts listed in the spreadsheet were converted to percentages.
- One possible explanation for the sharp decline in revenue from Jan to Feb is reduced consumer spending following the holiday season; however, additional market data would be required to confirm this.

### Limitations
- In the returns table, the quantity returned was not indicated. Additionally, many orders that were cancelled were presented in the dataset. To calculate the return rate, cancelled orders were excluded to ensure accuracy.
- Store locations were assigned numbers (e.g., Lagos 1–4) to prevent confusion because certain cities have more than one store, and using store IDs would be inconvenient and confusing.
- The supplier table did not have a foreign key in the other tables, hindering the creation of relationships. However, the supplier names correspond to brand name.


## Business Questions
#### 1.	Which product generated the highest revenue?
Home Item 400 generated the highest revenue.
#### 2.	Which store performed best?
The store that demonstrated the best performance was the STR015 (Lagos 3) store.
#### 3.	Which category sells the most?
Beauty products are the most popular among customers.
#### 4.	Which month generated the highest revenue?
The highest amount of revenue was generated in December, 2025.
#### 5.	Which customers spend the most?
Male customers spend more than female customers, and customers in the Gold membership tier generated the highest revenue.
#### 6.	Which products are frequently returned?
Home and beauty products have the highest return rates at 5.13% and 5.12%, respectively.
#### 7.	Which supplier supplies the highest number of products?
The Williams Group supplied the highest number of products (213 products), but Martinez Ltd. generated the highest amount of revenue (₦ 29,879,705.60 ).
#### 8.	Which stores have low inventory?
The Kaduna 2, Abuja 4, Enugu, Ibadan 3, and Abuja 1 stores have the low inventory.
#### 9.	Which region performs best?
Stores in the southern region displayed the best performance during the analysis period, with the East coming in second.
#### 10.	Which product category should management invest in?
Beauty, Home and Fashion. Management should invest in beauty products, as these are the most popular products among customers. Therefore, it is important to ensure that stock is always available. However, home and fashion products are the lowest-selling product categories. As a result, management should invest in improving marketing campaigns to draw customers attention toward these product categories.

## Key Findings
- Revenue increased by 2% during 2025.
- Beauty products contributed 23% of total revenue.
- Southern region stores generated 32% of total revenue.
- Kaduna stores experienced the highest inventory shortages.
- Beauty and Home products recorded the highest return rates.

## Recommendations and Conclusion

- Increase marketing efforts in underperforming regions.
- Improve inventory management in high-shortage stores.
- Ensure best-selling products remain adequately stocked.
- Investigate late deliveries contributing to product returns.

This analysis demonstrates that the performance of ShopSmart Retail Ltd stores may be linked to inventory availability, holiday spending habits, marketing efforts, and regional conditions. Acting on these insights and recommendations will allow ShopSmart to maximize revenue and increase store and product visibility. Further analysis is required to evaluate customer demographic information based on gender, age, and location in relation to sales, revenue generation, inventory effects, and return rates. Additionally, a review in six months should be considered to evaluate the results of implementing these insights.
